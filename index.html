A living science, five thousand years in the making.

What is Ayurveda?

Ayurveda — “the knowledge of life” — is India’s traditional system of wellbeing, practised for over five thousand years. It sees health as a dance between body, mind and surroundings, and offers daily and seasonal rhythms to keep the three in harmony. Not a quick fix: a way of living well, slowly and on purpose. Among its eight classical branches sits vajikarana — the dedicated science of desire, intimacy and reproductive vitality — the ground KAMVASA calls home.

OUR STORY

Old roots,
new rhythms.

KAMVASA began between two worlds — the courtyard kitchens and herb baskets of an Indian childhood, and the fast, bright pace of the cities we grew up in. Somewhere in between, intimacy lost its everyday wisdom: crowded out by stress, screens and silence. We decided to carry the old knowledge forward instead.

So we build slowly: drawing on the vajikarana and rasāyana traditions, working with growers and practitioners, formulating with honesty and testing with rigour. Shame-free and whisper-free — every jar is an invitation to take ten minutes back for yourself, and for the people you love.
